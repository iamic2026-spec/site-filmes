## Brand & Style

The design system projects an uncompromisingly immersive, high-voltage theatrical atmosphere. Built for discerning cinephiles and digital natives, the design balances the pitch-black drama of an auditorium with luminous, hyper-vibrant neon accents.

### Design Movement & Language
The aesthetic integrates **Glassmorphism** and **Cinematic High-Contrast Cyber-Editorial**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep obsidian canvas planes layered with translucent glass, dramatic vignette gradients, and colored specular glow (neon spill).
- **Tactile Vibrancy:** Interactive cues behave like laser-lit cinema marquees—energized by electric magenta and warm gold highlights.
- **Editorial Presence:** Large-scale, poster-inspired typography anchors the canvas, establishing a premium prestige-entertainment feel without sacrificing operational readability.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for a low-light, high-immersion theater environment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Neutrals:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0A0A0E` (Screen backdrop and root surface).
  - Elevated Container / Glass Base: `#12121A` with deep twilight undertones (`#10172A`).
  - Ghost Borders & Separators: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)`.
  - Content Text: `#F8FAFC` (High emphasis), `#94A3B8` (Medium emphasis), `#64748B` (Muted/Captions).

- **Primary Accent (Neon Magenta / Hot Electric Rose):**
  - Anchor: `#F43F5E` (with glowing tints up to `#FF1493` and `#EC4899`).
  - Role: Primary calls-to-action, playback progression, focus states, and marquee badges.

- **Secondary Accent (Marquee Cinema Gold):**
  - Anchor: `#F59E0B` (illuminated variant `#FBBF24`).
  - Role: Critical critical acclaim scores, VIP/Premiere memberships, laurel wreaths, and ticketing highlights.

- **Tertiary Accent (Atmospheric Cyan / Neon Blue):**
  - Anchor: `#38BDF8`.
  - Role: Technical audio/video metadata badges (4K HDR, Dolby Atmos, IMAX Enhanced).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear tension between dramatic poster titles and razor-sharp digital precision.

- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** Boasts dramatic, avant-garde geometric structures. Used for featured hero film titles, section headers, and premiere spotlights. It evokes classic 70mm and modern neo-noir poster billing.
- **Body & Editorial (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, warm, contemporary geometric sans designed for legibility at reading lengths across synopses, reviews, cast listings, and settings.
- **Labels, Badges & Meta (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical, slightly monospaced character shapes used exclusively for metadata (durations, ratings, audio codecs, tags, and micro-navigation). Always displayed uppercase in `label-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an edge-to-edge cinematic orientation with an immersive fluid 12-column grid.

### Layout Adaptability
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin-desktop` (3.5rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Film carousels peek off-canvas to invite horizontal discovery. Hero spotlights break gutters using 100vw banners with localized text bounds.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Media cards reflow into 3-column rows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (0.75rem). Carousels switch into fluid horizontal snap-scroll rails with trailing card cutoffs.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical section-to-section flow relies on generous macro pauses (`space-xl` scaled dynamically) to mimic trailer pacing, while metadata clusters within cards remain tightly bundled with `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through light emission and optical refraction rather than traditional opaque drop shadows.

### Glass Surfaces (Backdrop Filter)
- **Glass Base:** `background: rgba(18, 18, 26, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a continuous hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Glass:** `background: rgba(16, 23, 42, 0.8)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and `border: 1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.

### Chromatic & Ambient Glow
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Default):** No shadow; relies entirely on the contrast between `#12121A` and `#0A0A0E`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Card Hover / Floating Tray):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modal / Overlays):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`.
- **Spotlight Highlights:** Backdrop pseudo-elements render radial gradient flares (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15), transparent 70%)`) above featured content.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances screen proportions with soft modern rounding.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for standard input fields, badges, action chips, and nested inner controls.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** The standard for film poster cards, trailer preview boxes, popups, and floating glass menus.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Reserved for hero banners, modal sheets, and the main video player control chassis.
- **Pill Exception:** Pure circular or pill bounds (`rounded-full`) are applied exclusively to icon action buttons (e.g., Play, Add to Watchlist, Mute) and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero CTA (Play / Resume):** Filled `#F43F5E` with bold white text. On hover, transitions to a neon bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.6)`) and a scale transform of `1.02`.
- **Secondary Action (Trailer / Info):** Frosted glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), solid white text. Hover initiates a border color change to `#FBBF24` with gold glow.
- **Icon Actions (Watchlist, Share):** 44px circular frosted buttons with centered vector icons; hover triggers primary accent fill.

### Film & Series Cards
- **Structure:** 2:3 vertical poster aspect ratio or 16:9 backdrop ratio.
- **Default State:** Sharp poster visual, subtle inner gradient shadow at bottom edge, subtle 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Hover State:** Smooth scale (`scale-105`), z-index jump, illuminated neon magenta rim border (`1px solid #F43F5E`), and bottom glass tray expansion unveiling quick-play controls and rating badges.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- **Cinema Gold Tag (Rotten Tomatoes / IMDb / Premiere):** Deep amber-tinted background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), text `#FBBF24`, hairline border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Tech Spec Chip (4K, Atmos, HDR10+):** Dark slate surface (`#10172A`), uppercase text `#38BDF8`, subtle cyan tint.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Style:** Glass trench style (`rgba(10, 10, 14, 0.8)` background, inset `0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
- **Focus State:** Stroke transition to glowing magenta (`#F43F5E`) with `0 0 12px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.

### Checkboxes & Switch Controls
- **Track/Base:** Dark neutral `#1E293B`.
- **Active State:** High-saturation `#F43F5E` fill with soft pink halo.

### Media Player Scrubber (Progress Bar)
- **Track:** 4px tall `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Buffered Track:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Active Progress:** `#F43F5E` gradient into `#EC4899`, capped by an 8px circular thumb with an active magenta neon beacon.
